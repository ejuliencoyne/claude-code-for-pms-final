Prototype controls

These controls are not part of the app. They stand in for events the phone would get.
What to try
  1. On Home, read the "Recent activity" card and tap See recent pings.
  2. Send a ping, then tap Take it inside 60 seconds.
  3. Send another and let it run out (use "Skip to 5 seconds left"). See what the phone says. Click OK to clear it, or send another ping without clicking OK to see it stay on the next ping.
Numbers on the card are Meteor Mite's real weekly counts (1 ping in the week of 31 Aug; the last 10 pings are the real ones). The wording is proposed, not built.
