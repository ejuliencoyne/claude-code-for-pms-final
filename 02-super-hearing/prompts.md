# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.
can you give a summary of what people are unhappy about in interviews?

### 2.
the important issues are 1 and 2, can you now look at the support tickets and give additional info about those two issues?

### 3.
do we have data about locations of responders and pings?

### 4.
can you see how proximity worked in the routing algorithm before 4.2?

### 5.
can you give me a table of ping data for all of the responders who have submitted tickets in issue 2?

### 6.
can you give a list of responders in each area?

### 7.
where is the app getting travel time from?

### 8.
can you add the gale to the tables above?

### 9.
does each individual ping event have an identifier so we can see where each ping went if it wasn't picked up?

### 10.
can you show every callout that included the gale in the week of 24 aug?

### 11.
what would make a responder count as completely unavailable? interested in callouts 41181 and 41193 and why no one else was pinged

### 12.
let's move back to just the interviews and tickets - can you compare those and see what is inconsistent between them?
