What happened

Two changes shipped together, and a rule nobody had revisited since 2019 did the rest.

Before 12 AugPing wait 90 s. Taken rate flat at 75–78% for six weeks. Support: 5–8 tickets a week.
12 Aug: release 4.2Ping wait cut to 60 s and proximity weighted up over recent acceptance.
Mid-AugMisses jump from ~2% to 12–21%. Four responders miss most of what they get, then stop being pinged.
Early SeptAggregate taken rate recovers to 73%, partly because the four dropped out of the numbers.
The mechanism (from the routing code)

A missed ping counts as a decline (−0.12) while a taken ping earns only +0.08, and scores never decay (a 2019 TODO). Once a responder drops down the order they get fewer pings, so they have almost no way to earn the score back.

What we can't explain

Why these four missed 59% of pings after 12 Aug while the other twelve missed 14%. There's no device or answer-time data in the pings table, so this stays an open question rather than a finding.

Module 5 · Super Speed · the fix

Decay old misses, soften the penalty, and tell handlers when a responder's pings drop.

Routing
Scores decay (~14-day half-life) and a miss costs less than a turn-down (e.g. −0.06 vs −0.12). The 60 s wait stays.
Handlers
A "Receiving fewer pings" flag when the last 7 days fall under 50% of the prior 4-week average, plus an 8-week ping report.
Responders
A "Recent activity" card, and expired pings that say what happened until the responder clicks OK.

Where this stands

What's settled, and what's still open.

Settled
  • The step is on 12 Aug, and the 60 s wait raised misses for most responders.
  • A miss costs more than a take earns and nothing decays, so a low score is hard to climb out of.
  • Aggregates hid four responders for weeks; track acceptance per responder.
  • The fix keeps 60 s and adds decay, a lighter miss penalty, a handler flag and a report.
Open
  • Why the four miss 59% of pings. No device or answer-time data exists.
  • Decay and the lighter penalty are untested; replay on the pings data first. Decay alone left the four's replayed scores very low.
  • Support has no tool to reset a responder's score yet.
  • Data stops 6 Sep, so I can't confirm whether anyone has recovered since.
  • The one-pager needs a named owner and the reset tool brought into its scope.
